## 🚨 **COMMON PITFALLS TO AVOID**

### **Technical Pitfalls**
- Don't get lost in technical details early
- Avoid jargon without explanation
- Don't demo broken features
- Don't apologize for "work in progress"

### **Presentation Pitfalls**
- Don't rush the problem statement
- Avoid reading from script verbatim
- Don't undersell the impact
- Don't forget to breathe and smile

### **Content Pitfalls**
- Don't claim unrealistic market capture
- Avoid vague future promises
- Don't diminish competitors unfairly
- Don't ignore obvious limitations

## 🎤 **HANDLING Q&A LIKE A PRO**

### **Likely Questions & Answers**

**Q: "How accurate is the AI simplification?"**
**A:** "We leverage Google's Gemini AI, which has been trained on vast legal datasets. Our testing shows 95%+ accuracy in clause interpretation, and we include confidence scores for transparency."

**Q: "What's your monetization strategy?"**
**A:** "Freemium model - basic simplification free, advanced features like risk scoring and negotiation tips are premium. B2B licensing to legal platforms and real estate companies."

**Q: "How do you handle legal liability?"**
**A:** "We position as an educational tool, not legal advice. Clear disclaimers, and we're exploring partnerships with licensed legal professionals for validation."

**Q: "What about privacy and security?"**
**A:** "Enterprise-grade security with JWT authentication, encrypted data transmission, and optional local processing. Documents can be processed without storage."

**Q: "How is this different from existing tools?"**
**A:** "Existing tools just translate - we provide clause-by-clause analysis, risk assessment, and personalized insights based on your role. Plus, we're built specifically on Google's Gen AI."

### **If You Don't Know Something**
- "That's a great question - we haven't implemented that yet, but here's how we'd approach it..."
- "I'd love to explore that with you after the presentation"
- "That's definitely on our roadmap for the next iteration"

\noindent \textbf{PARTIES:} This Lease Agreement (``Agreement'') is entered into on September 1, 2025, between METRO PROPERTIES LLC, a limited liability company organized under the laws of the State of California (``Landlord''), and SARAH JOHNSON (``Tenant'').

\vspace{0.2cm}
\noindent \textbf{PREMISES:} Landlord hereby leases to Tenant and Tenant hereby leases from Landlord the premises located at 1425 Oak Street, Apartment 3B, San Francisco, CA 94102 (``Premises''), together with all appurtenances thereto.

\vspace{0.2cm}
\noindent \textbf{TERM:} The term of this lease shall commence on September 15, 2025, and shall continue for a period of twelve (12) months, terminating on September 14, 2026, unless sooner terminated pursuant to the provisions hereof.

\vspace{0.2cm}
\noindent \textbf{RENT:} Tenant agrees to pay Landlord monthly rent in the amount of Three Thousand Five Hundred Dollars (\$3,500.00), payable in advance on or before the first day of each calendar month. Rent for any partial month shall be prorated on a daily basis.

\vspace{0.2cm}
\noindent \textbf{SECURITY DEPOSIT:} Upon execution of this Agreement, Tenant shall deposit with Landlord the sum of Seven Thousand Dollars (\$7,000.00) as security for the faithful performance of Tenant's obligations hereunder. Said deposit may be applied by Landlord to cure any default of Tenant, including but not limited to unpaid rent, damages to the Premises beyond ordinary wear and tear, or cleaning expenses upon termination. The security deposit shall not be deemed prepaid rent and shall not bear interest unless required by law.

\vspace{0.2cm}
\noindent \textbf{USE AND OCCUPANCY:} The Premises shall be used and occupied solely as a private residential dwelling by Tenant and members of Tenant's immediate family, and for no other purpose whatsoever without the prior written consent of Landlord. Occupancy by guests for more than fourteen (14) consecutive days or more than thirty (30) days in any twelve-month period shall constitute a breach of this provision.

\vspace{0.2cm}
\noindent \textbf{PETS:} No animals, birds, or pets of any kind shall be kept on or about the Premises without the prior written consent of Landlord. If such consent is granted, Tenant agrees to pay an additional monthly pet fee of One Hundred Fifty Dollars (\$150.00) and an additional pet security deposit of Five Hundred Dollars (\$500.00).

\vspace{0.2cm}
\noindent \textbf{MAINTENANCE AND REPAIRS:} Tenant acknowledges that the Premises are in good order and repair and agrees to return said Premises to Landlord in the same condition, ordinary wear and tear excepted. Tenant shall be responsible for all repairs and replacements necessitated by Tenant's negligence or misuse. Landlord shall maintain the structural elements of the building and shall make repairs to major systems including plumbing, electrical, and heating, provided that the need for such repairs is not caused by the negligent or wrongful act of Tenant.

\vspace{0.2cm}
\noindent \textbf{ALTERATIONS:} Tenant shall not make any alterations, additions, or improvements to the Premises without the prior written consent of Landlord. Any alterations, additions, or improvements made to the Premises shall become the property of Landlord and shall remain upon the Premises at the expiration or termination of this lease unless Landlord requests their removal.

\vspace{0.2cm}
\noindent \textbf{ASSIGNMENT AND SUBLETTING:} Tenant shall not assign this lease or sublet the Premises or any part thereof without the prior written consent of Landlord, which consent may be withheld in Landlord's sole discretion. Any attempted assignment or subletting without such consent shall be void and shall constitute a material breach of this Agreement.

\vspace{0.2cm}
\noindent \textbf{COVENANT OF QUIET ENJOYMENT:} Landlord covenants that upon Tenant's payment of rent and performance of all terms and conditions hereof, Tenant shall peaceably and quietly have, hold, and enjoy the Premises during the term of this lease, subject to the terms and conditions hereof.

\vspace{0.2cm}
\noindent \textbf{DEFAULT:} If Tenant fails to pay rent when due and such failure continues for three (3) days after written notice, or if Tenant breaches any other provision of this Agreement and fails to cure such breach within thirty (30) days after written notice (or such shorter period as may be required by law), then Landlord may terminate this lease and re-enter and repossess the Premises. In the event of such termination, Tenant shall remain liable for all rent and other charges accrued to the date of termination, plus the costs of reletting the Premises.

\vspace{0.2cm}
\noindent \textbf{HOLD HARMLESS:} Tenant agrees to indemnify and hold harmless Landlord from any and all claims, damages, costs, and expenses, including reasonable attorneys' fees, arising from Tenant's use of the Premises or from any activity, work, or thing done, permitted, or suffered by Tenant in or about the Premises.

\vspace{0.2cm}
\noindent \textbf{FORCE MAJEURE:} Neither party shall be liable for any failure or delay in performance under this Agreement which is due to fire, casualty, flood, earthquake, elements of nature, acts of God, acts of war, terrorism, epidemics, quarantine restrictions, strikes, labor disputes, or other causes beyond the reasonable control of such party.

\vspace{0.2cm}
\noindent \textbf{GOVERNING LAW:} This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of the State of California. Any legal action arising out of this Agreement shall be brought in the courts of San Francisco County, California.

\vspace{0.2cm}
\noindent \textbf{ENTIRE AGREEMENT:} This Agreement contains the entire agreement between the parties and may not be modified except by written agreement signed by both parties. If any provision of this Agreement is held to be invalid or unenforceable, the remainder of the Agreement shall remain in full force and effect.

\section*{ADDENDUM A: ADDITIONAL TERMS AND CONDITIONS}

\noindent \textbf{LATE FEES:} If rent is not received within five (5) days after the due date, Tenant agrees to pay a late fee of One Hundred Dollars (\$100.00) plus Ten Dollars (\$10.00) for each additional day the rent remains unpaid.

\vspace{0.2cm}
\noindent \textbf{UTILITIES:} Tenant shall be responsible for payment of all utilities including electricity, gas, water, sewer, trash collection, cable, and internet services. Landlord shall not be liable for interruption of any utility services.

\vspace{0.2cm}
\noindent \textbf{PARKING:} One (1) designated parking space is included with the rental of the Premises. Additional parking spaces may be available for an additional monthly fee of Seventy-Five Dollars (\$75.00) per space, subject to availability.

\vspace{0.2cm}
\noindent \textbf{NOISE ORDINANCE:} Tenant agrees to comply with all noise ordinances and shall not permit any unreasonably loud or disturbing noises or activities that may disturb other tenants or neighbors, particularly between the hours of 10:00 PM and 7:00 AM.

\vspace{0.2cm}
\noindent \textbf{SMOKING POLICY:} Smoking of any substance is strictly prohibited anywhere on the Premises, including balconies and patios. Violation of this policy shall result in immediate termination of the lease and forfeiture of the security deposit.

\vspace{0.2cm}
\noindent \textbf{MOVE-OUT INSPECTION:} Upon termination of this lease, Landlord and Tenant shall conduct a joint inspection of the Premises within ten (10) days of Tenant's vacation. Any damages beyond ordinary wear and tear shall be documented and the cost of repairs shall be deducted from the security deposit.
